# religious-legal-systems

This example analyses five religious legal systems from a comparative and academic perspective using Legalis-RS, focusing on procedural structures, jurisdictional boundaries, and interfaces with secular law rather than doctrinal content. The systems covered are: Catholic Canon Law (1983 Code, marriage tribunal procedures), Islamic finance instruments (Sharia-compliant contracts widely used globally), Jewish personal status law (Israeli Rabbinical Court jurisdiction), Indian personal law (secular codification of religious family law), and Thai Sangha ecclesiastical organisation law.

## Usage

```sh
cargo run -p religious-legal-systems --all-features
```

## What It Demonstrates

- Canon Law marriage tribunal jurisdiction (Canon 1671, 1673) including 2015 Mitis Iudex reforms
- Islamic finance compliance rules modelled as GRANT/PROHIBITION statutes
- Jurisdictional boundary conditions (where religious law applies vs. secular courts)
- Interface rules between religious tribunals and civil recognition/enforcement
- Statute verification and audit trail across multi-tradition legal corpora

Part of [Legalis-RS](https://github.com/cool-japan/legalis)
